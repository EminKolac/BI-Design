# 1. Executive Summary — 1-Month Forward Strategy (March–April 2026)

## Macro Outlook for Turkiye

### CBRT Policy Rate Path
The Central Bank of the Republic of Turkiye (CBRT) held its benchmark one-week repo rate at **37.0%** in March 2026, pausing after five consecutive cuts from the October 2024 peak of 50%. The hold reflects caution around rising energy prices tied to Middle East conflict escalation. The CBRT suspended one-week repo auctions in early March, effectively lifting the interbank overnight rate ~300bps to near 40%. Consensus expects the policy rate to remain at 37% through Q2 2026, with gradual easing resuming in H2 toward a projected 12-month rate of ~28.5%.

### Inflation Trajectory
Annual CPI inflation registered **31.53%** in February 2026, up from the 30.65% January print. TurkStat's January 2026 methodology rebase (shifting to national accounts weights) mechanically pushed headline figures higher. The CBRT revised its 2026 year-end forecast midpoint upward from 16% to 18% (range: 15–21%). Market survey expectations sit at 25.4% year-end. ING forecasts 22% with upside risks. Disinflation is underway but slower than initially projected.

### TRY/USD Forecast
The lira traded at **44.17/USD** in mid-March 2026, near its all-time low. The CBRT sold >$8 billion in FX reserves in March's first week to defend the currency. Over the past 12 months, the lira has depreciated 21.1%. The managed depreciation policy (~2–3% monthly) is expected to continue. Year-end consensus: 46–48 TRY/USD.

## Sector-Level Strategic Priorities

### Energy (TPAO, BOTAS)
Natural gas exploration in the Black Sea (Sakarya field) remains the flagship project. Rising global energy prices due to Middle East tensions benefit TPAO's upstream revenues but pressure BOTAS's import costs. TVF is prioritizing energy investments that improve Turkiye's current account balance.

### Banking (Ziraat, Halkbank, Vakifbank)
State banks remain the backbone of TVF's portfolio (73% of AUM). Fitch upgraded outlook on four major Turkish banks in January 2026. The tight monetary environment compresses net interest margins short-term but supports asset quality. Halkbank's U.S. legal case is in its "final stretch" — a settlement could be a positive catalyst.

### Telecom (Turk Telekom, Turkcell, Turksat)
Turk Telekom delivered 50% revenue growth and 172% earnings growth in 2025. The stock hit ATH at TRY 75.65 in February before pulling back to ~59. 5G licensing discussions could be a major catalyst in 2026. Turkcell consolidation under TWF umbrella provides pricing power.

### Mining (Eti Maden)
Turkiye holds 73% of global boron reserves via Eti Maden. Rising demand for boron in EV batteries and advanced materials positions this as a long-term strategic asset. Lithium exploration projects ongoing.

### Petrochemicals & Insurance
Turkiye Sigorta (consolidated public insurer) benefits from inflation-driven premium growth. Istanbul Financial Center (IFC) project — TVF's flagship real estate investment — continues development.
